Three steps, and the middle one is just dropping a file.

  1. 1

    Pick a tool

    Browse the sidebar, or press / and type what you want to do. On a phone, the tool switcher sits right under the header.

  2. 2

    Drop your file

    Drag it in or tap to browse. Adding several? Drag them into the order you want before you run the tool.

  3. 3

    Download, or keep it

    Download the result straight away. If you have an account, save it to your vault instead.

Questions people ask first

Do I need an account?

No. Every tool works as a guest. An account only adds the cloud vault, where you can save results for up to 7 days.

Which formats can I use?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/iPhone photos, AVIF, TIFF, ICO), PDFs, Word documents (DOCX), video and audio files, ZIP archives, and text from scanned images. The QR generator exports PNG or SVG.

How long does the vault keep my files?

Seven days. Files expire automatically, and the vault holds up to 100 MB at a time.

What happens to files if I do not sign in?

Nothing is saved to a cloud vault. Recent files are listed locally in your browser, so you can find what you just processed.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to small screens, with a tool switcher under the header and large buttons for downloads.
